1. What cookies are

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of information saved on your device. They can make a site function, remember a choice, measure use or support advertising.

2. What we use

Essential storage

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser under acb-cookie-consent-v1. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, and does not require consent.

Tag management and consent signals

We use Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags. On its own it does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Google Consent Mode starts with analytics and advertising storage denied. Until you accept optional cookies, Google tags may send limited cookieless consent or measurement signals, but they must not read or write analytics or advertising cookies. Accepting optional cookies changes those consent settings to granted.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate visits and page performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first, says it does not collect or use visitors’ personal data, and does not track individual visitors across different Cloudflare customers’ sites. Its performance beacon therefore loads without relying on the optional-cookie choice.

Google Analytics (optional storage)

If you accept optional cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, including pages viewed, approximate location and device type. It may set cookies such as _ga for up to two years. When analytics storage is denied, Google states that identifiers are not stored in analytics cookies, although limited cookieless pings may still be sent under advanced Consent Mode.

Advertising (optional)

If you accept optional cookies, we use the Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Pixel and Google Ads tags to measure and improve our advertising on those platforms, including retargeting. These may set measurement and advertising cookies. They remain disabled until you accept.

3. Other third-party services

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms. It is used to deliver information you actively submit and is not an analytics cookie. Links or embedded services may be operated by third parties under their own policies. We do not intentionally permit optional analytics or advertising cookies before consent.

4. Your choices

You can accept optional cookies or continue with essential storage only. You can revisit the choice at any time.

You may also delete stored site data in your browser. Doing so means the banner may appear again.

5. Changes

We will update this page if the site begins using additional cookies or providers.
