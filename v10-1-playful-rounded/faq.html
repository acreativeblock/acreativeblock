Frequently asked questions

What is A Creative Block?

A space to understand why you keep getting stuck — and find your way back to the work. We coach creatives to the root of the block, then build the structure to move through it.

What exactly is a “creative block”?

Any pattern that keeps you from the work you want to do. It's rarely just laziness — underneath most blocks is a real reason: a fear, an unclear direction, a missing skill, or a life quietly working against you.

How is this different from productivity coaching?

Most coaching hands you a system and steps away. We start with the why — the root cause — and only then build the structure to move through it.

Is this coaching or consultancy?

Both. Getting stuck can be personal or practical — sometimes the block is about you (identity, fear, direction), and we coach; sometimes it's about the work itself (a project, a plan, a decision that won't settle), and we roll up our sleeves and consult. Most people need a little of each, and we move between the two as you do.

Do I have to be a professional creative?

No. This is for anyone with a project that matters — a book, an album, a side idea. You don't need to make a living from it to get stuck, or unstuck.

What happens in the first session?

We find which of the six blocks is really in your way, then map the first small steps. Naming it accurately is most of the work.

What if I'm a mix of several blocks?

Most people are, and that's normal. We find your primary one — the pattern with the most pull right now — and start there.

How do I get in touch?

Email hello@acreativeblock.com, or start with the free ‘Find your block’ questionnaire. We reply within a couple of days.
