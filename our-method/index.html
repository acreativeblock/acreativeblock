How we work

Understand the why.
Then move.

Five moves, in order: clarify what's actually going on, settle your state, address the real cause, form one concrete move, then test it in the work. Not a script—a way of reading what's happening before we respond to it.

01
Step 1 · Clarify

First, we clarify what's actually going on.

Every session starts here. We separate what you're carrying from what's actually stuck, and name the real mechanism—so we don't spend the session fixing the wrong thing.

What you're carrying

Mostly fixed

Personality

Stable traits shaping how you meet uncertainty, evaluation and unfinished work. We design around your wiring, never against it.

How your mind works

Attention, memory and processing differ from person to person. We work with your mind, not against a template of someone else's.

Mental-health context

Anxiety or low mood change the ground you work on. We stay in scope, and refer on when a block is really something else.

More movable

Motivation

Why the work matters, and whether the reason still pulls. Motivation can be rebuilt, reconnected, or honestly renegotiated.

Mood & energy

Your fuel on the day. Both flat and over-activated states narrow creative thinking—so we read it before pushing.

Environment

Time, space, interruptions, feedback and the people around the work. Often the fastest thing to change is the conditions, not the person.

Where it's stuck

The same stuck feeling can trace to very different causes. We name the mechanism—seven domains where creative work gets stuck, plus one look-alike that isn't a block at all—so we work on the real thing, not the surface.

02
Step 2 · Regulate

Then we settle your state.

Before anything else we read your state, because activation changes what the work needs right now. Too much narrows thinking into threat and rigidity; too little flattens it into disengagement.

Flooded

Pressure or threat is too high. The first move is to bring it down—not to demand more effort.

Empty

Fuel or engagement is too low. The first move is to restore capacity—not to push through.

Regulated

Enough activation to engage without overwhelm. This is where real creative work happens.

03
Step 3 · Address

Now we meet the real cause, not the symptom.

With the domain named and your state settled, we work directly on the actual project in front of you—not a hypothetical version of the problem. What this looks like depends on what's underneath: sometimes a structural fix to how the work is set up, sometimes a harder conversation about what the work means to you, sometimes just permission to do it badly first.

04
Step 4 · Form

You leave with one concrete move.

Insight on its own doesn't move a project. Every session ends with a single, specific next action—small enough to actually do this week, precise enough that you'll know whether it worked.

05
Step 5 · Test

Then you try it, and we adjust.

You test the move in the real work, not in the abstract. What happens next shapes the read, not just the plan—we keep adjusting until you can do this without us.
