Step 1

First, we look at what you're carrying.

Some of it is fixed, some of it can move—naming which is which keeps us from prescribing before we understand.

Mostly fixed

Personality

How you meet uncertainty and unfinished work.

How your mind works

Attention, memory, how you process ideas.

More movable

Motivation

Whether the reason to do this still pulls.

Mood & energy

Your fuel today—flat or over-activated.

Environment

Time, space, feedback, the people around it.

Step 2

...and how you are feeling.

Before anything else we read your state, because activation changes what the work needs right now.

Flooded

Pressure or threat is too high. The first move is to bring it down—not to demand more effort.

Empty

Fuel or engagement is too low. The first move is to restore capacity—not to push through.

Regulated

Enough activation to engage without overwhelm. This is where real creative work happens.

Step 3

...now, where is the work really stuck?

The same stuck feeling can trace to very different causes. We name the mechanism—seven domains where creative work gets stuck, plus one look-alike that isn't a block at all—so we work on the real thing, not the surface.
