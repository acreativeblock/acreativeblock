What we are

Most advice answers the wrong question—how to push harder. We start earlier, and treat the block as a symptom worth understanding before it's worth fixing. Coaching for the person, consulting for the project, and the honesty to say when it's something else.

Symptom & cause

The block is the symptom. The cause runs deeper.

Getting stuck isn't failure—it's information. Underneath most blocks sit perfectionism, exhaustion, or a self-worth fused to the work. We work on the cause, not just the surface.

It's rarely one thing.

The same stuck feeling can trace to very different drivers. We read the whole picture first.

How it works

Find your block. Read the terrain. Match the response.

01

Find your block

A short questionnaire, or a first conversation, points to the most likely mechanism—and a sensible first move.

02

Read the terrain & state

What's stable, what's movable, and whether you're flooded, empty or regulated. We read it before pushing for output.

03

Match the response

Coaching, consulting, learning, redesign or recovery—chosen for the real mechanism, not assumed.

Who it's for

For anyone bringing something into existence.

  • Artists and designers stuck mid-project.
  • Founders choosing what to build.
  • Researchers shaping an argument.
  • Writers facing the blank page.
  • Teams that have quietly stalled.

You don't need to call yourself an artist. If you make things, getting stuck is part of it—and there's a way through.
