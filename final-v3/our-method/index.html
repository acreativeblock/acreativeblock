First, we look at what you're carrying.

What's fixed

Temperament & wiring

Your personality, the way your attention and memory work, and your mental-health context. Stable ground—we design around it, never against it.

What can move

Motivation, energy & environment

Why the work matters, your fuel on the day, and the conditions around you—time, space, feedback, the people nearby. Usually the fastest thing to change.

Where you are today

Your state, right now

Too activated and everything reads as threat; too flat and nothing pulls. We read it before asking for output.
