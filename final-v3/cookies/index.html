Last updated: 11 August 2026. This policy explains cookies and similar browser storage used by acreativeblock.com.

1. What cookies are

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of information saved on your device. They can make a site function, remember a choice, measure use or support advertising.

2. What this beta currently uses

Essential storage

The site stores your cookie choice in your browser under acb-cookie-consent-v1. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies.

Optional analytics

No optional analytics or advertising cookies are activated by this beta implementation. If analytics such as Google Analytics, or advertising tools from Google or Meta, are added later, they must remain disabled until you actively accept optional cookies. This policy and the consent controls must then be updated with the provider, purpose and duration.

3. Third-party services

Links or embedded services may be operated by third parties under their own policies. We will not intentionally load non-essential third-party tracking before consent.

4. Your choices

You can accept optional cookies or continue with essential storage only. You can revisit the choice at any time.

You may also delete stored site data in your browser. Doing so means the banner may appear again.

5. Changes

We will update this page if the site begins using additional cookies or providers.

6. Contact

Questions can be sent to hello@acreativeblock.com.
