04

Method & evidence

Creative block is under-researched relative to how common it appears to be. There is no agreed scientific taxonomy, and some of the strongest evidence comes from writing, academic and artist samples. Our framework brings that evidence together with established theory and creative practice. It is an evidence-informed practitioner method—not a validated clinical diagnostic system.

Because “I am stuck” does not explain what needs to change. A working map helps us distinguish problems of action, identity, direction, energy, the work itself, systems, context and skill without pretending they are fixed diagnoses.

Yes. Blocks often overlap and can change over time. The map is used to organise enquiry, not reduce a person or project to one permanent category.

We use the best relevant research available, remain explicit about its limits, combine it with practitioner judgement and your real context, and revise the response according to what happens in practice.
