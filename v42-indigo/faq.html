Questions · 01–07

A few honest questions.

Q/01

What exactly is a creative block?

Any pattern that keeps you from the work you want to do. It's rarely just laziness — underneath most blocks is a real reason: a fear, an unclear direction, a missing skill, or a life quietly working against you.

Q/02

How is this different from productivity coaching?

Most coaching hands you a system and steps away. We start with the why — the root cause — and only then build the structure to move through it.

Q/03

Is this coaching or consultancy?

Both. Getting stuck can be personal or practical — sometimes the block is about you (identity, fear, direction), and we coach; sometimes it's about the work itself (a project, a plan, a decision that won't settle), and we consult. Most people need a little of each, and we move between the two as you do.

Q/04

Do I have to be a professional creative?

No. This is for anyone with a project that matters — a book, an album, a side idea. You don't need to make a living from it to get stuck, or unstuck.

Q/05

What happens in the first session?

We find which of the six blocks is really in your way, then map the first small steps. Naming it accurately is most of the work.

Q/06

What if I'm a mix of several blocks?

Most people are, and that's how it should be. We find your primary one — the pattern with the most pull right now — and start there.

Q/07

Where do sessions happen?

Online, everywhere — and in person around Lisbon. What matters is the work, not the room.

Q/08

What does it cost — and what if I can’t afford it?

We price on a sliding scale and we consider your income — and you never have to prove it, we trust you. We especially want to work with people who are underrepresented or usually priced out of this kind of support. If cost is the thing between you and starting, tell us and we’ll find a way. No one is turned away for lack of money.
