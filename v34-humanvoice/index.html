The taxonomy

Six kinds of stuck.

Most creative block is one of six patterns, with one that pulls hardest right now. Naming yours accurately is where the work begins.

01
Directional

Too many ideas, or none that pull. The block is a missing north — you need to know where, and why it's yours.

02
Structural

Work happens in bursts, then collapses. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going without willpower.

03
Execution

You know what to do and can't start — or finish. Usually fear in a productivity costume. We find what it protects.

04
Identity

"Am I even a real creative?" The block lives in the story about yourself, not the work.

05
Skill

The gap between your taste and ability is real, and it hurts. Not a flaw — a stage. We build a path across it.

06
Environmental

Your life, setup and people quietly work against the work. Sometimes the block is around you, not in you.

How we work

Root first, then a path.

1

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We name which of the six blocks is really in your way.

2

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced.

3

Build the way through

A clear path, gentle structure, and the accountability to keep going — so moving stops taking everything.

It's not a “just do it” problem. It's a disconnection — and no one moves through it alone.

Sometimes the block is about you; sometimes it's about the work. We coach and we consult, moving between the two as you do — with the community, accountability and space it actually takes to get unstuck.
