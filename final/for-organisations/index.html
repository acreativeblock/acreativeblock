How a team engagement goes

Diagnose, intervene, then make it stick.

We work on the project, the people and the system around them—so change outlasts a single day.

Step 1

Scope

With leadership: what is actually happening, what should change, and what a good outcome looks like.

Step 2

Diagnose

Short, candid conversations across the team. We locate the block at team level—without turning it into blame.

Step 3

Intervene

A workshop or working session on the live issue, ending with experiments, decisions and clear owners.

Step 4

Embed

Changes to briefs, feedback, roles or pace that survive past the room and into the everyday.

Step 5

Review

We check what held, adjust what did not, and hand over so the team can carry it themselves.
