01 · Terrain

What shapes whether you get blocked.

Terrain is the background creative work happens against. It changes how vulnerable you are to a block—but it is not the block itself.

Relatively stable

Personality, how your brain is wired, and mental-health context. We do not promise to erase these—we design around them and refer when appropriate.

More malleable

Motivation, mood and energy, and environment. These can often be influenced directly, or redesigned around.

Why it matters

Two people with the same block can need different support, because the terrain maintaining it is different.

02 · State

Flooded, empty, or regulated?

The same project can need opposite first moves depending on activation. State is a dial we read before pushing for output—not a sixth block.

Flooded

Pressure or threat is too high. The first move is to bring it down, not to demand more effort.

Empty

Fuel or engagement is too low. The first move is to restore capacity, not to push through.

Regulated

There is enough activation to engage without overwhelm. This is where real creative work happens.

03 · The session architecture

CRAFT—structure without a rigid recipe.

CRAFT organises the enquiry so it stays useful. (CRAFT the method is not "craft", the Process-domain problem.)

C

Clarify

What is stuck, what matters, and what may only look like a block?

R

Regulate

Make the problem workable before demanding output.

A

Address

Choose a response that fits the likely mechanism.

F

Form

Shape insight into a decision, experiment or practical change.

T

Test

Try it in real work, learn from the result and adjust.

Matching the response

Coach, consult—or combine carefully.

Coaching fits internal questions: fear, identity, direction, meaning. Creative consulting fits the work itself: briefs, decisions, structure, process, options. Many projects need both.

Other cases need something else entirely—learning a missing skill, redesigning a system, recovery, or referral. There is no honour in using the wrong tool consistently.
