5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data. We use service providers only where needed to operate the website and communicate with you. Current providers include Web3Forms, which processes contact-form submissions and forwards them by email; Google Forms and Google Sheets, which store a contact register containing the details submitted through the contact form; email and cloud tools used to receive and administer enquiries; Cloudflare Web Analytics for privacy-focused aggregate traffic and performance measurement; and Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel for analytics or advertising measurement in accordance with your cookie choice.

Web3Forms states that it does not retain form submissions, although temporary server logs may be kept for up to two months. A copy of an enquiry is retained in our email account and recorded in our contact register for follow-up, record keeping and, where you opted in, occasional updates. Each provider receives only the information needed for its role. Our Cookie Policy explains the website measurement tools and how to change your choice.

We may also share the minimum necessary information with professional advisers or authorities where required by law, needed to establish or defend a legal claim, or otherwise permitted under applicable law.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Portugal or the European Economic Area. Where required, we use an approved safeguard such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as needed for its purpose. Contact enquiries and related correspondence are normally kept for up to two years after the last meaningful contact. Brief working notes and service correspondence are kept for up to two years after the final session or service, then securely deleted or anonymised unless a longer period is needed for a live dispute, legal claim or legal obligation.

If you opt in to occasional updates through the contact form or a future newsletter form, we keep your email address, name, audience type and consent record until you unsubscribe or we close the list. We may retain the minimum information needed to honour an unsubscribe or objection. Booking, payment and invoice records are kept separately for the period required by Portuguese tax law, currently ten years. Analytics and advertising data follow the retention controls of the relevant provider and your cookie choice. We do not make or retain session recordings unless this is agreed with you in advance.

We use proportionate organisational and technical measures to protect confidentiality and security, although no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.
