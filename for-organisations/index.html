How we work with you

Coaching for the people, consulting for the system, often both. A stalled team is rarely one person to fix. We coach the people—naming the block at team level without turning it into blame—and we work on the conditions around them: briefs, decisions, roles, feedback and pace. Most of the time, it is a blend.
