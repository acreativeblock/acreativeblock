How we work with you

A stuck team isn't a discipline problem. It's rarely one person's fault—usually it's the conditions: unclear briefs, slow decisions, feedback that lands wrong. We look at the whole picture with you first, then work on what's actually holding people back, together, without turning it into blame. The fix sticks because everyone helped find it. Good work becomes possible again, and so does trusting each other while you make it.
