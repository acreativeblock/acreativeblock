What this is

A multi-week intervention for a team that has stalled. Leadership scoping, candid diagnostic conversations, focused team work and targeted coaching—changes to briefs, feedback, roles and pace that are built to outlast a single workshop.

What happens

  1. Scoping — Leadership conversations to understand what's really behind the stall.
  2. Diagnostic — Candid conversations across the team, not just with leadership.
  3. Intervention — Focused team work and targeted coaching on briefs, feedback, roles and pace.

Who it's for

Best for a team that has properly stalled, where the fix needs to touch more than one part of how the team works.
