What this is

A half or full day on a live issue. We identify the block at team level, agree what should actually change, and leave the room with experiments, decisions and clear owners—so momentum starts the same day.

What happens

  1. Before — A short scoping call to understand the live issue and who needs to be in the room.
  2. During — Half or full day working the issue directly with the team, not in the abstract.
  3. After — You leave with experiments, decisions and clear owners agreed before the room closes.

Who it's for

Best for one live, specific issue a team needs to move on quickly.
