About

Built from
lived experience

A Creative Block is built by someone who knows the territory first-hand — ADHD, unfinished projects, the loops of getting stuck. That makes the approach grounded, practical and genuinely kind.

Talent is rarely the problem. The block is — and it can be understood, eased, and moved through. That's the whole promise: from stuck to moving, with clarity on why, and a real next step.

What you can expect

Clear about what this is — and held with care.

Coaching, with care

Always clear scope

Grounded, not guru

Yours, in confidence
