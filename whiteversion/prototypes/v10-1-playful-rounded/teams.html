Practical, warm and tailored to your team — built around your real projects, not generic theory.

Team Workshops

Half or full-day sessions that get a stuck project or team moving — and give everyone a shared language for it.

Creative-team consulting

Hands-on help to untangle messy projects — scope, priorities and the workflow that protects creative focus.

An in-house creative counsellor on campus twice a month, helping students get unstuck and finish strong.

Why it helps

Creative teams underperform from friction and burnout, not lack of skill. Unblocking the work lifts both output and wellbeing — and it's a low-cost differentiator without hiring permanent staff.
