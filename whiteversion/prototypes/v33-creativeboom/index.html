The taxonomy

Six kinds of stuck — usually a mix.

Most creative block is one of six patterns, with one that pulls hardest right now. Naming yours accurately is where the work begins.

01

Directional

Too many ideas, or none that pull. The block is a missing north — you need to know where, and why it's yours.

02

Structural

Work happens in bursts, then collapses. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going without willpower.

03

Execution

You know what to do and can't start — or finish. Usually fear in a productivity costume. We find what it protects.

04

Identity

"Am I even a real creative?" The block lives in the story about yourself, not the work.

05

Skill

The gap between your taste and ability is real, and it hurts. Not a flaw — a stage. We build a path across it.

06

Environmental

Your life, setup and people quietly work against the work. Sometimes the block is around you, not in you.

Not a "just do it" thing

You don't get unstuck alone.

People move through blocks in relationship — with community around them, accountability that expects them to keep going, and a space that treats their creativity as worth the effort.
