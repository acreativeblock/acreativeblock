About · The practice

It exists because “just do it” never worked.

Not for our founder, and not for most creatives. The standard advice is aimed at the wrong problem — so we built a different one.

Our story

Getting unstuck is not a “just do it” problem.

A Creative Block began with a simple insight: it's deeper than that — often psychological, sometimes about identity, environment, or a quiet grief we never named. Treating it as a discipline failure is the same mistake as telling someone depressed to “just be happy.”

The practice grew out of founder Mirco's own experience — building things with ADHD, stalling for reasons that looked like laziness but never were, and slowly realising the standard advice was aimed at the wrong problem. What actually moved things wasn't more willpower. It was understanding, structure, and not being alone in it.

So that's what we offer: a place to understand the why beneath your block, and the community, accountability and partnership to move through it. Not slogans. Proper support.

What we believe
01

Block is a signal

Getting stuck isn't a flaw to fix. It's information about something real underneath. We listen to it before we try to move it.

02

Root before tactic

Systems only work once you know what they're for. We find the why first, then build the how around it.

03

No one moves alone

People get unstuck in relationship — with accountability, partnership, and a space that expects them to keep going.
