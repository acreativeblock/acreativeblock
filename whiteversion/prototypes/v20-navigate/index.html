Six blocks — and the why behind each

Most “stuck” is one of six patterns. Naming yours is where the work starts — each has a feeling, the honest signs, and the way through. Most of us are a mix, and that's completely normal.

01 · Directional
“I don't know what to make.”
Sounds like you if…
  • You start everything and finish nothing
  • You've lost the why behind it
  • Nothing really pulls at you anymore
The way throughCommit to one direction — to test, not forever.
02 · Structural
“It's pure chaos in here.”
Sounds like you if…
  • 40 tabs, scattered files, no real start
  • You keep redoing the same step
  • The plan only lives in your head
The way throughBreak the mess into three ordered steps.
03 · Execution
“I know what to do — I just don't.”
Sounds like you if…
  • You plan, then avoid the actual file
  • Starting feels weirdly impossible
  • Deadlines keep ambushing you
The way throughShrink it. Begin for ten ugly minutes.
04 · Identity
“What if I'm not good enough?”
Sounds like you if…
  • You stall right at the finish line
  • You can't hit publish or share
  • You compare yourself, constantly
The way throughShip it imperfect — you are not the work.
05 · Skill
“I don't know how to do the next bit.”
Sounds like you if…
  • You hit one technical wall
  • You binge tutorials instead of making
  • Impostor feelings creep in
The way throughName the one missing skill — learn just that.
06 · Environmental
“My life keeps killing my momentum.”
Sounds like you if…
  • You can't focus where you work
  • You're drained before you begin
  • Time and space fight you
The way throughChange one condition: space, time or energy.
