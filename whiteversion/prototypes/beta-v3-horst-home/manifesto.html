Manifesto · work in progress

Getting stuck is not a flaw. It is information.

A short manifesto for anyone who has mistaken a creative block for a character problem.

The spine

We were told the same thing, over and over: just do it. Push through. Stop making excuses.

For people who are genuinely stuck, that advice does not just fail—it adds shame to a problem that may never have been about willpower.

Stuck is information.

When you treat a block as a discipline problem, you attack the person instead of investigating the cause. The stuckness may point to action, confidence, direction, depleted capacity, the work itself, workflow or context. It may also be a concrete Skill Gap that needs learning.

One feeling. Different mechanisms.

Our working map contains seven true domains plus one important look-alike. They overlap, change over time and do not define who you are. Naming the mechanism creates clarity; shifting it requires a response that fits.

We start with the why, then work on what moves it.

Sometimes that means coaching. Sometimes direct creative consulting, system redesign, learning, recovery or referral. There is no honour in using the wrong tool consistently.

For anyone bringing something into existence.

You do not need to call yourself an artist. Founders, researchers, strategists, students and teams all use creativity when they turn possibility into something real.
