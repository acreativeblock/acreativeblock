How it feels

You're not lazy, and you're not broken. Something specific is in the way, and it deserves a proper look, not another pep talk.

  • You keep preparing instead of starting.
  • Too many ideas, no clear priority.
  • Motivation has quietly drained away.
  • Feedback knocked your confidence.
  • The work no longer feels like yours.
How we support you

We sit with you first—what's actually happening, what it's costing you, what would make you feel like yourself again—then we move together, at a pace that respects where you're starting from. This isn't about trying harder. It's about getting unstuck, and liking the work again.
