How it feels

Creative work can get stuck when it matters most. You might keep preparing, circling the project, second-guessing every move, or watching something that once felt alive become strangely hard to touch. We have been there. You are not lazy, and you are not broken. Usually, something specific is in the way—and it deserves a proper look, not another pep talk.

How we support you

Work on the person, the project, or both.

We begin with what is happening in the work and in you. From there, we choose the right response: coaching when the pattern is personal, consulting when the project needs a clearer way through, or a mix when they are tangled together.

  1. 01 · Start with what is happeningBring the work as it is

    Not a tidy summary—the actual project, in whatever state it is in.

  2. 02 · Find the real blockSeparate symptom from cause

    We look at the work, the context and the experience around it.

  3. 03 · Choose the right toolCoaching, consulting, or both

    We match the work to what you need rather than forcing one method.

  4. 04 · Test a way forwardMake a small move in the real project

    We turn the insight into an experiment you can take back to the work.

  5. 05 · Build a way to continueKeep moving without us

    We review, refine and leave you with a way to carry it forward.
