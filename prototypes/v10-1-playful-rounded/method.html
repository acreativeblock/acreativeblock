The method

How we help
you move

A simple, repeatable way to get unstuck — built on what actually helps, not willpower or pressure. Whatever's in the way, there's a path through it.

Find what's in the way→ Ease the friction→ Take the smallest step→ Build momentum

What a session feels like

Calm, practical and judgment-free. You leave with clarity and one real next step — never a lecture.

We find it together

We listen for what's really getting in the way — not just the surface symptom.

We clear the friction

We gently remove what's quietly blocking you and shrink the work to something doable.

You leave with a step

One small, concrete next action — clear enough that you can actually start.

Momentum builds

A light accountability loop turns small steps into finished work — and confidence.

What we draw on

Six lenses we bring together to understand a block and find the kindest way through it.

How focus, dopamine and the nervous system shape creativity — and why ADHD minds get stuck differently. Understanding it takes the shame out of it.

Perfectionism, comparison and self-worth — and how to grow a kinder, more flexible relationship with your work.

Emotional regulation, rest as fuel, and recovering from burnout — so you can create from a steadier place.

Ideation, exploring vs. committing, and working with constraints — plus honest, encouraging skill-building.

Gentle structure, accountability and ADHD-friendly ways of working — where good intentions finally become finished work.

Reconnecting with why you create, and finding your people — because momentum is so much easier together.
