What we mean

A creative block is any pattern that keeps you from the work that matters.

It's rarely just laziness. Underneath most blocks is a real reason — a fear, an unclear direction, a missing skill, or a life quietly working against you. Treating it as a discipline problem is the same mistake as telling someone anxious to “just relax.”

Often what looks like “not being creative anymore” is an unprocessed loss: people rarely quit in one dramatic moment — they disengage slowly, over years. We take that seriously, and give it the tools it actually needs: inquiry, identity work, and a way back to the work.

How we work · The method
01

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We name which of the six blocks is really in your way.

02

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced.

03

Build the way through

A clear path, gentle structure and the accountability to keep going — so moving stops taking everything.

Coaching when it's about you. Consultancy when it's about the work.
