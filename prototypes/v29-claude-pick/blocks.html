The six blocks

Six ways of being stuck. Usually, a few at once.

Most stuck is one of six patterns. Naming yours is where the work begins — each has a feeling, honest signs, and a way through.

What kind of stuck are you?

Whatever's in the way today, there's a way through. Move through the six, one at a time.

Block 01

Directional

Too many roads, none chosen. You're not short on ideas — you have too many, and choosing one feels like losing the rest. So you circle, and call it stuck.

The way throughCommit to one direction — to try, not forever.

Block 02

Structural

The mess is the hiding place. It isn't that you don't want to work — you can't find the edge to begin at. Tidying feels like progress while the real decision waits.

The way throughBreak the mess into three ordered steps.

Block 03

Execution

You know. You just don't. The moment you go to begin, something pulls the handbrake — avoiding brings quick relief, and the mind has learned to take it. It isn't laziness.

The way throughShrink it, and begin for ten small minutes.
Find your block →
Block 04

Identity

Finishing feels like a verdict. When the work is a test of whether you're enough, an unfinished piece feels safer than a judged one — so you stall at the last stretch.

The way throughLet it be imperfect. You are not the work.
Find your block →
Block 05

Skill

A gap, not a flaw. There's one thing the work needs that you haven't learned yet. Left unnamed, it grows into “maybe I'm not enough.”

The way throughName the one missing skill — and learn just that.
Find your block →
Block 06

Environmental

The setup quietly wins. Willpower can't out-muscle a life that spends your energy before you reach the work. It isn't you — it's the room.

The way throughChange one condition: space, time, or energy.
