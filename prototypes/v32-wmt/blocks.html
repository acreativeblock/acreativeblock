Six kinds of stuck

Most creative block is one of six patterns — and most people are a mix, with one that pulls hardest right now. Naming yours accurately is where the work begins.

The Six BlocksTaxonomy
01
Directional

The tell: too many ideas, or none that pull. You can't tell which thing is yours to make.

The block here is a missing north. Before you can move, you need to know where — and why it matters to you specifically.

Clarity & direction
02
Structural

The tell: work happens in bursts, then collapses. Momentum never compounds.

You know what you want, but nothing holds it. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going that doesn't run on willpower.

Systems & scaffolding
03
Execution

The tell: you know exactly what to do and still can't start — or can't finish.

Usually this is fear wearing a productivity costume. We find what it's protecting, so starting stops feeling like a threat.

Starting & finishing
04
Identity

The tell: "am I even a real creative?" The doubt lives in the story about yourself, not the work.

When identity is the block, no amount of tactics moves it. We work on the story until the work has room to exist.

Self & story
05
Skill

The tell: the gap between your taste and your ability is real, and it hurts.

This isn't a flaw — it's a stage everyone passes through. We build a path across it that you'll actually walk.

Craft & growth
06
Environmental

The tell: your life, setup and people are quietly working against the work.

Sometimes the block isn't in you at all — it's around you. We change the conditions instead of blaming you for them.

Context & conditions

You don't need to fit one box. You need to name the one that's loudest.

We find your primary block — the pattern with the most pull today — and start there. The others get easier once the first one moves.
