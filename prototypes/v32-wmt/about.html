Why This ExistsThe Story

The story

A Creative Block began with a simple insight: getting unstuck is not a "just do it" problem. It's deeper than that — often psychological, sometimes about identity, environment, or a quiet grief we never named. Treating it as a discipline failure is the same mistake as telling someone depressed to "just be happy."

The practice grew out of founder Mirco's own experience — building things with ADHD, stalling for reasons that looked like laziness but never were, and slowly realising that the standard advice was aimed at the wrong problem. What actually moved things wasn't more willpower. It was understanding, structure, and not being alone in it.

So that's what we offer: a place to understand the why beneath your block, and the community, accountability and partnership to move through it. Not slogans. Proper support.
