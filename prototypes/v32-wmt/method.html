The Method

Root first, then a path

We don't hand you a system and step away. We start with the why beneath the stuck, then build the structure to move through it — and we stay in it with you.

Three Moves01 — 03
01

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We name which of the six blocks is really in your way — accurately, not conveniently.

02

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity, loss — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced or shamed away.

03

Build the way through

A clear path and gentle structure, plus the accountability and space to keep going. Moving forward stops taking everything you've got.

What A Session Looks LikeIn Practice

In practice

The first session is diagnostic in the gentlest sense: we talk, and together we find which block has the most pull right now. Most of the work is naming it correctly — people often arrive certain it's discipline, and leave realising it was fear, or direction, or grief.

From there we work in a rhythm: short, focused sessions to keep the why in view, small doable steps between them, and honest check-ins so nothing quietly slips back into avoidance. You're not handed a worksheet and left alone. You're accompanied.

Coaching when it's about you. Consultancy when it's about the work.

Sometimes the block is personal — identity, fear, direction. Sometimes it's practical — a project, a plan, a decision that won't settle. Most people need a little of each, and we move between the two as you do.
