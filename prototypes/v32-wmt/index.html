We help you understand why you keep getting stuck

A Creative Block is a coaching & consultancy practice for creatives who keep stalling on the work that matters. Getting stuck isn't a flaw — it's a signal.

We name what's underneath the stuck, then build the structure to move through it. Founded from lived experience with ADHD and the mess of making things.

The Six Blocks01 — 06
01
Directional

You're not sure what to make, or why. Too many ideas, or none that pull. The block here is a missing north — before you can move, you need to know where.

Clarity & direction
02
Structural

You know what you want, but there's no system holding it. Work happens in bursts and then collapses. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going without willpower.

Systems & scaffolding
03
Execution

You know exactly what to do and still can't start — or can't finish. Usually this is fear wearing a productivity costume. We find what it's protecting.

Starting & finishing
04
Identity

"Am I even a real creative?" The stuck lives in the story about yourself, not the work. When identity is the block, no amount of tactics moves it.

Self & story
05
Skill

The gap between your taste and your ability is real and it hurts. This block isn't a flaw — it's a stage. We build a path across it that you'll actually walk.

Craft & growth
06
Environmental

Your life, your setup, your people are quietly working against the work. Sometimes the block isn't in you at all — it's around you. We change the conditions.

Context & conditions
How We WorkThe Method
01

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We start by naming which of the six is really in your way.

02

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced.

03

Build the way through

A clear path and gentle structure, so moving forward stops taking everything you've got.

Coaching when it's about you. Consultancy when it's about the work.

Sometimes the block is personal — identity, fear, direction. Sometimes it's practical — a project, a plan, a decision that won't settle. Most people need a little of each, and we move between the two as you do.
