Which one sounds like you?

Six familiar kinds of stuck — each with a feeling, a few honest signs, and the way through. Most of us are a mix, and that's completely normal.

Option A · Grid
01 · Directional
“I don't know what to make.”
Sounds like you if
  • You keep exploring but never commit
  • You've lost the why behind it
  • Everything feels equally meh
The way throughPick one direction you can test this week.
02 · Structural
“It's a chaos in here.”
Sounds like you if
  • 40 tabs, scattered files, no clear start
  • You keep redoing the same step
  • The plan only lives in your head
The way throughTurn the mess into three ordered steps.
03 · Execution
“I know what to do — I just don't.”
Sounds like you if
  • You plan, then avoid the actual file
  • Deadlines keep sneaking up
  • Starting feels weirdly impossible
The way throughShrink it. Start for ten ugly minutes.
04 · Identity
“What if I'm not good enough?”
Sounds like you if
  • You stall right near the finish
  • You won't hit publish or share
  • You compare yourself constantly
The way throughShip it imperfect. You aren't the work.
05 · Skill
“I don't know how to do the next bit.”
Sounds like you if
  • You're stuck at a technical step
  • You binge tutorials instead of making
  • Impostor feelings creep in
The way throughName the one missing skill — learn just that.
06 · Environmental
“My life keeps killing my momentum.”
Sounds like you if
  • You can't focus where you work
  • You're drained before you begin
  • Time and space fight you
The way throughChange one condition: space, time or energy.
