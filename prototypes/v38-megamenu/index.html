It's not a “just do it” problem.

Most advice treats a psychological event as a scheduling error. We don't. A block can be about identity, environment, fear or an old, unnamed loss — and it deserves proper attention, not a slogan. We treat it as real, and give it the tools it actually needs.

The taxonomy

Six kinds of stuck — usually a mix.

Most creative block is one of six patterns, with one that pulls hardest right now. Naming yours accurately is where the work begins.

01
Directional

Too many ideas, or none that pull. The block is a missing north — you need to know where, and why it's yours.

Clarity
02
Structural

Work happens in bursts, then collapses. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going without willpower.

Systems
03
Execution

You know what to do and can't start — or finish. Usually fear in a productivity costume.

Starting
04
Identity

“Am I even a real creative?” The block lives in the story about yourself, not the work.

Self
05
Skill

The gap between taste and ability is real, and it hurts. Not a flaw — a stage. We build a path across it.

Craft
06
Environmental

Life quietly works against the work. Sometimes the block is around you, not in you.

How we work

Root first, then a path.

1

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We name which of the six blocks is really in your way.

2

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced.

3

Build the way through

A clear path, gentle structure and the accountability to keep going — so moving stops taking everything.

Coaching when it's about you. Consultancy when it's about the work.

Sometimes the block is personal — identity, fear, direction. Sometimes it's practical — a project, a plan, a decision that won't settle. Most people need a little of each, and we move between the two as you do — with the community, accountability and space it actually takes.
