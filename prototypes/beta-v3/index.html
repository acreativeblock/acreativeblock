Creative block is real

Common enough to matter.
Complex enough to investigate.

Creative block is not laziness or a lack of discipline. It can come from identity, direction, energy, process, systems or the context around the work—and different causes need different responses.

78%

reported experiencing creative block in one survey of 271 professionally active artists and writers.*

*That number describes this specific sample, not every creative profession. Creative block remains under-researched; we use the evidence carefully and say where its limits are.
