A different kind of help

Talent is rarely the problem. The block is — and there's a way through it.

We don't push harder or wait for inspiration. We gently work out which of six familiar blocks is in your way, ease the friction around it, and turn it into one doable next step — so the work starts moving again.

The six blocks

Which one sounds like you?

01 · Directional

“I don't know where to go.”

Usually not motivation — just no clear direction yet. The way through: a small, doable direction to aim at.

Read more →
02 · Structural

“The project's a chaos.”

Big vision, tangled plan. The way through: turn it into clear, ordered, do-able steps.

Read more →
03 · Execution

“I know what to do — I just don't.”

Not laziness — your mind is protecting you. The way through: shrink it, and add a little accountability.

Read more →
04 · Identity

“Am I actually good enough?”

Finishing can feel like a verdict. The way through: let the work be separate from your worth — and imperfect.

Read more →
05 · Skill

“I don't know how to do the next part.”

Sometimes you just need the skill. The way through: name it, and learn the smallest piece.

Read more →
06 · Environmental

“My life keeps killing my momentum.”

Often the problem's around you, not in you. The way through: redesign the conditions — time, space, energy.
