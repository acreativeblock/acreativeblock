How we work

Understand the block. Match the response.

We do not use one tool for every problem. We read the terrain, current state and active mechanism before deciding what kind of help fits.

01 · Terrain

What shapes whether you get blocked.

Terrain is the background against which creative work happens. It changes vulnerability to particular blocks, but it is not itself the active block.

Relatively stable

Personality, how your brain is wired, and mental-health context. We do not promise to erase these. We design around them and refer when appropriate.

More malleable

Motivation, mood and energy, and environment. These can often be influenced directly or redesigned.

Why it matters

Two people with the same active block may need different support because the terrain maintaining it is different.

02 · State

Flooded, empty or regulated?

The same project can require opposite first moves depending on activation. Flooded means pressure or threat is too high. Empty means fuel or engagement is too low. Regulated means there is enough activation to engage without overwhelm.

State is a dial, not another block type. We read it before pushing for output.

03 · Active block

Which mechanism is holding the work?

We use seven overlapping domains plus a Skill-Gap look-alike. The domain names create a useful hypothesis; they do not define the person or automatically dictate the intervention.

The session architecture

The CRAFT method.

CRAFT structures the enquiry without turning it into a rigid recipe. CRAFT, the method, is not “craft,” a Process-domain problem.

C

Clarify

What is stuck, what matters, and what may only look like a block?

R

Regulate

Make the problem workable before demanding output.

A

Address

Choose a response that fits the likely mechanism.

F

Form

Shape insight into a decision, experiment or practical change.

T

Test

Try it in real work, learn from the result and adjust.

Matching the response

Coach, consult—or combine carefully.

Coaching fits internal questions such as fear, identity, direction and meaning. Creative consulting fits the work itself: briefs, decisions, structure, process and options. Many projects need both. Other cases need learning, system redesign, recovery or referral.

Before naming a block

Able + willing + stuck + suffering.

We first ask whether there is enough basic capability, genuine intention to create, inability to begin or continue, and meaningful distress or consequence. That avoids turning rest, incubation, changed priorities or ordinary difficulty into a diagnosis.
