Research · The factors

The block is only one layer.

How you tend to operate, the conditions around the work and your state today all shape what becomes difficult—and what kind of response can help.

The terrain

Design around what is stable. Work on what can move.

Stable does not mean fixed forever. Malleable does not mean easy to change. The distinction helps us stop blaming people for their wiring—and stop treating changeable conditions as destiny.

01 · Relatively stable

The background you bring.

Longer-standing patterns that shape vulnerability. We understand and design around them rather than trying to “fix” the person.

Personality

Temperament, sensitivity to evaluation and preferences for novelty, certainty or structure.

Cognition

Attention, working memory, processing style and neurodivergence in complex creative work.

Mental-health context

Relevant background that can affect the work and may require qualified support beyond our scope.

02 · More malleable

The conditions we can influence.

Factors that may shift through decisions, recovery, experimentation, boundaries or system redesign.

Motivation

Meaning, autonomy, reward, conflict and whether the project still has enough pull.

Mood & energy

Available fuel, stress load, recovery and the emotional conditions around the task.

Environment

Time, space, tools, feedback, relationships, decision rights and practical support.
