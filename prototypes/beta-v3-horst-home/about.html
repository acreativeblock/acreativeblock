About / the founder

Creativity became clearer when the definition got wider.

A Creative Block was founded by Mirco Fragomena to give stuck creative work the thoughtful attention that “just do it” never could.

Founder story

My block was direction—not an inability to make.

For years, I tried to understand myself through a traditional idea of being creative: the artist who produces recognisably artistic work. What kept returning was a sense that I was creative but somehow doing it incorrectly.

The shift came from recognising that my strongest creative abilities were analytic, strategic and systemic: listening closely, seeing patterns, organising complexity, connecting people and ideas, and helping projects find a workable route.

That experience matters here because creative block is not limited to a blank canvas. A founder choosing what to build, a strategist unable to commit to a direction, or a researcher trying to shape an argument is also bringing something into existence.

Why the practice exists

Understanding before prescription.

Creative difficulty is too often met with slogans, shame or universal productivity advice. This practice starts with a different question: what is actually maintaining the stuckness?

Evidence-informed

Research and established theory guide the enquiry, with clear language about uncertainty and limits.

Human and practical

The person matters, and so do the brief, workflow, decision, environment and work itself.

Non-woo

No manifestation language, heroic myths or promise to unlock a hidden genius—just careful attention and useful work.

Approach & background

Building expertise transparently.

A Creative Block is an emerging practice grounded in years of creative and strategic work, lived experience of creative block, structured research and continuing coach training.

The framework is a practitioner synthesis, not a validated clinical diagnostic system. The work develops through research, supervision, conversations with creatives and organisations, and honest review of what helps in practice.
