The research we draw on

A multidisciplinary synthesis.

Creative block is under-researched relative to how common it appears to be. There is no agreed scientific taxonomy, and some of the strongest evidence comes from writing, academic and artist samples.

Our framework brings together creative-block research, psychology, coaching, creative practice and systems thinking. Adjacent research on motivation, self-efficacy, mood, avoidance, fixation, incubation, environment and feedback helps us ask better questions without pretending it directly validates our map.

What we can—and cannot—claim

A useful practitioner method, not a clinical diagnosis.

We can use the framework to organise observation, form working hypotheses and match proportionate responses. We cannot claim it is a validated diagnostic instrument, that its domains are the final scientific taxonomy, or that one label determines treatment.

Boundaries mean scope

Not everything difficult is a creative block.

A specific missing capability may be a Skill Gap: teach or learn it. Exhaustion may require recovery. A clinical or medical concern requires an appropriately qualified professional. We do not rename these issues to keep them inside our service.
