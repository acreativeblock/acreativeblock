Getting unstuck is possible.

A Creative Block is coaching & consultancy for creatives who keep stalling on the work that matters. We find the why beneath the block — then help you move.

Our belief
Getting stuck isn't a flaw. It's a signal — something underneath asking for attention. It's not a “just do it” problem.

Most advice treats a psychological event as a scheduling error. We don't. We treat the block as real, and give it the tools it actually needs.

The taxonomy

Six kinds of stuck — usually a mix, with one that pulls hardest.

01

Directional

Too many ideas, or none that pull. The block is a missing north — you need to know where, and why it's yours.

Clarity & direction
02

Structural

Work happens in bursts, then collapses. The block is scaffolding — a way to keep going without willpower.

Systems & scaffolding
03

Execution

You know what to do and can't start — or finish. Usually fear in a productivity costume. We find what it protects.

Starting & finishing
04

Identity

“Am I even a real creative?” The block lives in the story about yourself, not the work.

Self & story
05

Skill

The gap between taste and ability is real, and it hurts. Not a flaw — a stage. We build a path across it.

Craft & growth
06

Environmental

Life quietly works against the work. Sometimes the block is around you, not in you. We change the conditions.

Context & conditions

How we work

Root first, then a path — and we stay in it with you.

01

Find the root

The stuck you feel is rarely the stuck that's there. We name which of the six blocks is really in your way.

02

Understand the why

We look at what the block protects — fear, meaning, identity — so it loosens its grip instead of being forced.

03

Build the way through

A clear path, gentle structure and the accountability to keep going — so moving stops taking everything.

Ways to work together

1:1 coaching

Format B

Intensives

A focused deep-dive for a specific, stuck project or decision — closer to consultancy, when you need to move something now.
