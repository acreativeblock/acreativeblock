Questions

The honest ones.

Do I have to be a professional creative?

No. This is for anyone with a project that matters — a book, an album, a side idea. You don't need to make a living from it to get stuck, or unstuck.

Is this coaching or consultancy?

Both. Sometimes the block is about you (identity, fear, direction) and we coach; sometimes it's about the work itself and we consult. Most people need a little of each.

What if I can't afford it?

We price on a sliding scale, consider your income, and never ask you to prove it. No one is turned away for lack of money.

Where do sessions happen?

Online, everywhere — and in person around Lisbon. What matters is the work, not the room.
