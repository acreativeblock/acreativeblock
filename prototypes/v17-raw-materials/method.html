The method

Get to the why — then move

Most coaching stops at accountability. We start by finding why you keep getting stuck — usually psychological, not structural — then build the structure and steps to actually move through it.

Find what's in the way→ Ease the friction→ Take the smallest step→ Build momentum
What a session feels like

Calm, practical, judgment-free

01

We find it together

We listen for what's really getting in the way — not just the surface symptom.

02

We clear the friction

We gently remove what's quietly blocking you, and shrink the work to something doable.

03

You leave with a step

One small, concrete next action — clear enough that you can actually start.

04

Momentum builds

A light accountability loop turns small steps into finished work — and confidence.

Or a different kind of session

Not every session starts from stuck. Come in with a half-formed idea, a project you want to develop, or a direction you're unsure about — and leave with clarity, a structure, or simply a better sense of what you're actually making.
