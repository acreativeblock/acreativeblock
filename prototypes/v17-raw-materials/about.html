About

Built from lived experience

Built by someone who's been stuck first-hand — ADHD, unfinished projects, the loops of avoidance. I work on why the block keeps coming back, not just accountability — then the structure to move through it. Practical, honest, and genuinely kind.

Hello

A person, not a programme

I'm Mirco. I came to this as a designer — years of making things, shipping some, and quietly sitting on others because releasing them felt too exposing. ADHD made the loops louder: too many directions, big ideas, and a strange inability to start the obvious thing. I learned this work because I needed it myself first.

That design background shapes how I see your work — structure, craft and taste, the why behind a choice — not a generic productivity lens. I care how a thing looks and feels, and how an idea actually becomes real.

And honestly, I just love thinking through creative problems with people. Not every session needs to start from a place of stuck — sometimes you've got a half-formed idea and want a sharp, friendly brain to develop it with for an hour. That's some of my favourite work.
