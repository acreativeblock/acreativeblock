Research & method

A block never comes
from nowhere.

Six things shape whether the same project leaves you stuck or moving. Three you are mostly born with or carry for a while; three you can actually change. Knowing which is which is where the work starts.

Mostly fixed

Three you carry with you.

We do not promise to erase these. We design around them, and refer on when that is the right call.

Personality

Stable traits shape how you approach uncertainty, evaluation and unfinished work. You cannot out-discipline your own wiring—and you should not try to.

How your brain works

Attention, memory and the way you process ideas differ from person to person. The aim is to work with your mind, not against a template of someone else's.

Mental-health context

Anxiety, low mood and related conditions change the terrain. We stay in scope, and point you to the right professional when a block is really something else.

More movable

Three you can actually shift.

This is where most of the practical work happens—directly, or by redesigning what surrounds the work.

Motivation

Why the work matters, and whether the reason still pulls. Motivation can be rebuilt, reconnected, or honestly renegotiated.

Mood & energy

Your state on the day—flooded, empty or regulated—changes what is possible. We read it before pushing for output.

Environment

Time, space, interruptions, feedback and the people around the work. Often the fastest change is to the conditions, not the person.

Why it matters

Same block, different terrain, different fix.

Two people can arrive with the identical stuck feeling and need opposite first moves—because the terrain maintaining it is different. Reading the factors keeps us from prescribing before we understand.
