Seven true domains where creative work gets stuck—plus one important look-alike that is not a block at all. This map is the heart of how we work. Follow any door to see what it looks like and what tends to move it.

Before the label

A domain is a hypothesis, not a diagnosis.

The names create a useful starting guess—they do not define you or automatically dictate what we do next. Most real situations touch more than one door, and change over time.

That is why we read your state first—flooded, empty or regulated—before deciding what the work actually needs.
