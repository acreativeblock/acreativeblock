The framework

Creative block isn't one thing. It's six.

01 · Directional

"I don't know where to go."

Not motivation — direction.

Helps: constraints; an actionable, not permanent, direction.

02 · Structural

"The project is chaos."

Vision, broken architecture.

Helps: restructure scope, stages, priorities.

03 · Execution

"I avoid doing it."

Not laziness.

Helps: shrink the task, remove ambiguity, add accountability.

04 · Identity

"Am I good enough?"

A verdict on your worth.

Helps: separate self-worth from output.

05 · Skill

"I don't know how."

Misread as emotional.

Helps: name the missing skill, smallest path to it.

06 · Environmental

"My context kills it."

The problem is outside.

Helps: redesign conditions — sleep, schedule, space.
