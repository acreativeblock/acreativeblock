Every block is a different animal

Getting moving again starts with knowing which one you're dealing with.

Directional

You're not sure what to make, or which way to take it.

Structural

The project feels too big or chaotic to get a grip on.

Execution

You avoid starting, or aren't sure what the next step is.

Identity

Self-doubt — impostor feelings, unsure of your voice.

Skill

A specific technique or ability you haven't learned yet.

Environmental

No time, distractions, or an uninspiring space to work.
