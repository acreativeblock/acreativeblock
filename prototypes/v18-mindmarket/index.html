A few patterns behind why you get stuck

Most “stuck” is one of six patterns. Scroll each one — the feeling, and the way through. Most of us are a mix; the work is finding your primary driver.

Find your block
Directional
01

Too many roads, none chosen

You don't lack ideas — you have too many, and picking one feels like killing the rest.

The way throughCommit to one direction — to test, not forever.
Structural
02

The mess is the hiding place

You can't find the edge to start at, so tidying quietly feels like progress.

The way throughBreak the mess into three ordered steps.
Execution
03

You know — you just don't

Avoiding brings instant relief, and your brain has learned to take it. Not laziness.

The way throughShrink it. Begin for ten ugly minutes.
Identity
04

Finishing feels like a verdict

An unfinished piece is safer than a judged one, so you stall at the last ten percent.

The way throughShip it imperfect — you are not the work.
Skill
05

One real gap, ballooning

A vague gap becomes “maybe I'm not good enough,” so you avoid naming the missing piece.

The way throughName the one missing skill — learn just that.
Environmental
06

The setup fights you

Willpower can't out-muscle a life that spends your energy before you reach the work.

The way throughChange one condition: space, time or energy.
