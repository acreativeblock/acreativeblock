Which one sounds like you?

Six familiar kinds of stuck — and a way through each. Most of us are a mix, and that's completely normal.

01 · Directional

“I don't know where to go.”

Usually it's not motivation — just no clear direction yet. The way through: a small, doable direction to aim at.

02 · Structural

“The project's a chaos.”

Big vision, tangled plan. The way through: turn it into clear, ordered, do-able steps.

03 · Execution

“I know what to do — I just don't.”

Not laziness — your mind is protecting you from something. The way through: shrink it, and add a little accountability.

04 · Identity

“Am I actually good enough?”

Finishing can feel like a verdict on you. The way through: let the work be separate from your worth — and a little imperfect.

05 · Skill

“I don't know how to do the next part.”

Sometimes you simply need the skill. The way through: name it, and learn the smallest piece that unlocks the rest.

06 · Environmental

“My life keeps killing my momentum.”

Often the problem is around you, not in you. The way through: redesign the conditions — time, space, energy.
