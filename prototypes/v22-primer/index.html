Find your block

Every creative deserves a way through.

The building blocks of getting unstuck.

We draw on the real reasons creatives stall — and turn them into a path. Most “stuck” is one of six patterns; naming yours is where the work starts.

01

Directional

Too many roads, none chosen. You don't lack ideas — you have too many, and picking one feels like killing the rest.

02

Structural

The mess is the hiding place. You can't find the edge to start at, so tidying feels like progress.

03

Execution

You know — you just don't. Avoiding brings instant relief, and your brain has learned to take it. Not laziness.

04

Identity

Finishing feels like a verdict. An unfinished piece is safer than a judged one, so you stall at the last ten percent.

05

Skill

One real gap balloons into “maybe I'm not good enough,” so you avoid naming the missing piece.

06

Environmental

Your setup spends your energy before you reach the work. Willpower can't out-muscle the wrong room.
