The framework

Creative block isn't one thing.
It's six.

A block is a symptom — the cure depends on the cause.

01 · DIRECTIONAL

"I don't know where to go."

Not lack of motivation — lack of direction.
Mechanism
Commitment anxiety — choosing one path closes others.
What helps
Constraints; an actionable, not permanent, direction.
02 · STRUCTURAL

"The project is chaos."

Vision and drive, but broken architecture.
Mechanism
The brain can't process undefined complexity.
What helps
Restructure scope, stages, priorities, dependencies.
03 · EXECUTION

"I avoid doing it."

This is not laziness.
Mechanism
The nervous system tags the task as threatening.
What helps
Shrink the task, remove ambiguity, add accountability.
04 · IDENTITY

"Am I good enough?"

The project becomes a verdict on your worth.
Mechanism
Finishing feels dangerous, so you stall near the end.
What helps
Separate self-worth from output; normalise imperfect release.
05 · SKILL

"I don't know how."

Often misdiagnosed as emotional.
Mechanism
The project exceeds current ability → shame, avoidance.
What helps
Name the missing skill and the smallest path to it.
06 · ENVIRONMENTAL

"My context kills it."

The problem is outside, not inside.
Mechanism
Environment incompatible with sustained creative work.
What helps
Redesign conditions: sleep, schedule, boundaries, space.
