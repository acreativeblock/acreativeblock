Behind the map: what shapes whether you get blocked

Blocks don’t form in a vacuum — they sit on your terrain. Some of that is relatively fixed; a lot of it is changeable. It’s why we don’t give everyone the same advice.

Fairly fixedHow your mind tends to work — temperament, cognitive style, and any mental-health context. We design around these, not against them.
ChangeableMotivation, energy and mood, and the environment around the work. This is where most of the movement comes from.
