About

We help creatives get to the root of why they keep getting stuck. Since 2015 we've worked with makers, writers and whole studios — finding the real block underneath the procrastination, then building the structure to actually move through it. We stay in it with you, before, during and after the work.

We work with everyone from solo makers to whole teams — you don't have to do this for a living to qualify.

The work isn't just diagnosis. We help you name the block, understand what it's protecting, and build a plan you'll actually follow.

The six blocks

Directional

Too many roads, none chosen. You're not short on ideas — picking one feels like killing the rest, so you circle.

Find your block →

Structural

The mess is the hiding place. You can't find the edge to start at, so tidying quietly feels like progress.

Find your block →

Execution

You know — you just don't. Avoiding brings instant relief, and your brain has learned to take it. Not laziness.

Find your block →

Identity

Finishing feels like a verdict. An unfinished piece is safer than a judged one, so you stall at the last ten percent.

Find your block →

Skill

A gap, not a flaw. One specific thing the work needs that you haven't learned — so it balloons into self-doubt.

Find your block →

Environmental

Your setup eats your focus. Space, hours and noise spend your energy before you ever reach the work.

How we work
1

Find the block

We figure out which of the six patterns is really in your way — naming it accurately is most of the work.

2

Understand the why

We look at what the block is protecting — fear, identity, meaning — so it loses its grip instead of returning.

3

Build the plan

A clear path and the right systems, matched to your block — doable steps, not a generic framework.

4

Move with support

Gentle accountability while you actually begin — we stay in it with you, not waving from the sidelines.

5

Finish it

We keep you moving to the finish line, and set you up to get unstuck on your own next time.
