Method · Coaching

MAKE ROOM FOR THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK.

Coaching helps when fear, identity, confidence, direction, meaning or internal conflict is keeping the work stuck.

What coaching does

THINK MORE CLEARLY WITHOUT HAVING THE ANSWER IMPOSED.

Coaching uses reflection, challenge and carefully chosen questions to help you notice what is happening and develop a way forward you can genuinely own.

It is useful when the work has become tangled with self-worth, exposure, competing values, uncertainty or protective avoidance.

When coaching may lead

WHEN THE OBSTACLE LIVES INSIDE THE EXPERIENCE OF DOING THE WORK.

These often overlap. They are starting points for enquiry, not boxes to put someone in.

01

Direction & meaning

Clarifying what matters, what to choose and which trade-off you are willing to make.

02

Confidence & exposure

Working with fear of judgement, impostor feelings, voice and the risk of being seen.

03

Starting & doing

Understanding what avoidance protects you from and making approach more possible.

A clear boundary

COACHING IS NOT THERAPY, INSTRUCTION OR A DISGUISED ANSWER.

It does not treat clinical conditions or replace qualified mental-health care. It also cannot teach a missing technical skill. When the project needs direct expertise, creative consulting—or a considered blend—may fit better.
