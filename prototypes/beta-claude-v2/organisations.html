THE PERSON IS ONLY PART OF THE PICTURE.

Creative work is shaped by briefs, feedback, decision rights, workload, psychological safety and the conditions in which people are expected to think.

We combine facilitation, creative consulting and coaching to work on the project, the people and the system around them. The aim is not another inspiring afternoon. It is a useful change in how the work moves.
