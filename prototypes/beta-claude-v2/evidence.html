The ground beneath the method

A MULTIDISCIPLINARY SYNTHESIS.

Creative block is under-researched relative to how common it appears to be. There is no agreed scientific taxonomy, and some of the strongest evidence comes from writing, academic and artist samples.

What informs the method

RESEARCH HELPS US ASK BETTER QUESTIONS.

Our framework brings together creative-block research, psychology, coaching, creative practice and systems thinking.

  • Motivation and meaning
  • Self-efficacy and identity
  • Mood, energy and avoidance
  • Fixation and incubation
  • Environment and feedback
Where the evidence stops

ADJACENT EVIDENCE IS NOT DIRECT VALIDATION.

Related fields sharpen observation, but they do not prove that our map is the final or only way to classify creative block.

  • No agreed scientific taxonomy
  • Uneven samples and professions
  • No validated 7+1 diagnostic test
  • Domains can overlap and change
  • Labels do not determine treatment
What we can claim

A USEFUL PRACTITIONER METHOD.

The framework can organise observation, form working hypotheses and help match a proportionate response to what appears to be happening.

What we cannot claim

NOT A CLINICAL DIAGNOSIS.

It is not a validated diagnostic instrument, its domains are not the final scientific taxonomy, and one label cannot determine treatment.

Boundaries mean scope

NOT EVERYTHING DIFFICULT IS A CREATIVE BLOCK.

A specific missing capability may be a Skill Gap: teach or learn it. Exhaustion may require recovery. A clinical or medical concern requires an appropriately qualified professional.

We do not rename these issues to keep them inside our service.
