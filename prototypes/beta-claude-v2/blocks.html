Research · The block map

A block is the experience. Not the cause.

A creative block is a meaningful inability to begin or continue work you are able and willing to do. The same stuck feeling can come from different mechanisms—and each needs a different response.

The block map

THE 7+1

Seven overlapping domains, plus one important look-alike. Open any row to explore the mechanism, signs and useful forms of support.

01

Starting & Doing Execution block

You know the next move, but avoid, delay, restart or stop before showing it.

 02

Confidence & Exposure Identity block

The work feels like a judgement of your ability, identity or worth.

 03

Direction & Meaning Direction block

There are too many directions—or none with enough meaning or pull.

 04

Energy & Recovery Depletion block

The work asks for attention and capacity that are not currently available.

 05

The Work Itself Process block

The idea, brief, material or approach keeps producing the same dead end.

 06

Structure & Workflow Systems block

Scope, sequencing, ownership or workflow repeatedly recreates friction.

 07

Context & Support Context block

Time, space, feedback, relationships or support are fighting the work.

 +1

A Skill Gap Look-alike—not a true block

A useful boundary

WHAT IS NOT A BLOCK?

Not every pause, struggle or unfinished project is a creative block. It may be incubation, chosen rest, ordinary difficulty, a changed priority, lack of willingness, or a specific skill you have not learned yet.

The distinction matters because these situations need permission, a decision, practice or teaching—not an intervention for creative block.
