Method · Creative consulting

BRING ANOTHER MIND INTO THE WORK.

Creative consulting helps when the obstruction sits in the brief, idea, decisions, structure, workflow, feedback or working conditions.

What consulting does

WORK DIRECTLY ON THE PROJECT AND THE SYSTEM AROUND IT.

Consulting is collaborative and direct: challenge assumptions, reframe the problem, generate options, simplify scope and clarify decisions.

You remain responsible for the project. The value is informed challenge and practical thinking—not handing over ownership.

When consulting may lead

WHEN THE WORK—OR WHAT SURROUNDS IT—NEEDS ATTENTION.

We work on the live issue rather than forcing the project into a generic process.

01

The work itself

A weak brief, narrow framing, fixation or creative approach that is not yielding.

02

Structure & workflow

Oversized scope, unclear sequencing, reopened decisions and unnecessary friction.

03

Context & support

Feedback, roles, time, environment or team conditions that keep obstructing progress.

Often a blend

THE PROJECT AND THE PERSON AFFECT EACH OTHER.
