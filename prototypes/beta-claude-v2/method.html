Method · CRAFT

TURN UNDERSTANDING INTO MOVEMENT.

CRAFT is the working sequence we use to move from a vague stuck feeling to a proportionate response, then learn from what happens in the real work.

The working sequence

CRAFT

Clarify. Regulate. Address. Forward. Test.

The sequence is clear but not mechanical. New information can send us back to an earlier step and change the working hypothesis.

C

Clarify

Name the project, what matters, what is happening instead, and what may only look like a creative block.

R

Regulate

Check whether the person is flooded, empty or regulated and make the problem workable before demanding output.

A

Address

Choose the response that fits: coaching, consulting, redesign, learning, recovery or referral.

F

Forward

Move insight into a decision, practical change or experiment that can carry the work ahead.

T

Test

Try the response in real work, notice what changes and revise the hypothesis when needed.

Method, not diagnosis

CRAFT ORGANISES THE WORK. IT DOES NOT LABEL THE PERSON.

The block map helps us form a hypothesis. The Factors page helps us read terrain and state. CRAFT structures the engagement once we have enough clarity to respond.

CRAFT, the method, is not “craft,” a Process-domain problem concerning the work itself.
