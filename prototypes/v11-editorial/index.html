Which one sounds like you?

Six familiar kinds of stuck — and a way through each. Most of us are a mix, and that's completely normal.

01 · Directional
“I don't know where to go.”
Not a lack of motivation — just no clear direction yet. The way through: a small, doable aim.
02 · Structural
“The project's a chaos.”
Big vision, tangled plan. The way through: clear, ordered, do-able steps.
03 · Execution
“I know what to do — I just don't.”
Not laziness — your mind is protecting you. The way through: shrink it, add accountability.
04 · Identity
“Am I actually good enough?”
Finishing can feel like a verdict. The way through: separate the work from your worth.
05 · Skill
“I don't know how to do the next part.”
Sometimes you simply need the skill. The way through: learn the smallest piece that unlocks the rest.
06 · Environmental
“My life keeps killing my momentum.”
Often the problem is around you, not in you. The way through: redesign the conditions.
