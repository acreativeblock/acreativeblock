Our approach

Creative blocks need more than “just do it” or “push harder”. When work gets stuck, it is easy to keep forcing the same approach instead of stepping back to see what has gone out of view. A block can involve what is relatively stable—your personality, strengths, aptitude, values and vision—as well as what shifts day to day: motivation, mood, energy and how you are feeling. We use research to understand the pattern, then tailor the work so you can engage without being pushed into overwhelm.
