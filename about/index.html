About A Creative Block

A Creative Block is a coaching and consulting practice for people and teams whose meaningful work has stopped moving. Being stuck is rarely proof that you lack ideas, talent or discipline. It can come from the project, the process, the conditions around it, or the version of “creative” you have been trying to become. We look for the mechanism before suggesting the move—then work with the person, the project, or both.

In isolation. Some problems need a second mind, useful feedback or simply someone who understands.

Why we exist

The block is rarely about the work.

Most advice says to start badly, play more or push through. Sometimes that is exactly right. Sometimes the project needs a clearer process, a boundary, recovery or better working conditions.

The useful question is not “what is wrong with me?” but “what is keeping this stuck now?”

Too many people leave meaningful work while specific support remains surprisingly thin. That gap is why we are here.

The story

Fifteen years of looking in the wrong direction.

A Creative Block grew from Mirco’s long attempt to become the kind of creative he thought he should be. Product design became graphic design, then music, photography, documentary, writing, apps and more systems for finding the answer. The work changed; the feeling that something was missing did not.

The turn came from dropping the question “what should I become?” and noticing what was already consistent: people brought him their unfinished projects, difficult decisions and tangled ideas. He could see patterns, make a room feel safe and help another person find a way through.

The block was not a lack of creativity. It was a narrow definition of where creativity was allowed to live.

Creativity is not a medium. It is bringing something into being with what you actually have.

What we believe

A few things we will not ask you to pretend.

Good support makes the situation clearer without turning the block into a character flaw.

01

You do not have to fit the costume. Creative is not a look, a personality type or a list of approved disciplines.

02

Your block deserves an investigation, not a slogan. Different mechanisms need different responses.

03

Nobody has to unblock alone. A second mind can change what becomes visible.

04

Your brain is not broken; the system might be. Sometimes the useful shift is practical.

05

You will be supported, not shamed. We work with how you actually think and make.
